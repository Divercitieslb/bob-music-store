# Image prompts — Bob Music Store

Nine images carry the whole site. Everything else is product photography, which
is already done.

**House style, true of every prompt below.** Keep it consistent or the site will
look like a stock-photo collage:

> Warm, low-key natural light from one side. Deep shadows, no flat fill, no
> studio softbox look. Palette limited to near-black (#14100C), warm brown
> (#6C4824), brass (#B0854A) and bone (#EFE6D6) — no blues, no greys, no cool
> tones. Shot on a full-frame camera, 35mm or 50mm, f/2, shallow depth of field.
> Fine film grain. Photorealistic, editorial, unposed. No text, no logos, no
> watermarks, no visible brand names.

**Where each one goes.** Upload in Shopify: *Customize → click the section →
Image*. Filenames are only a suggestion — Shopify renames on upload.

A note on people: unposed hands and half-turned figures work; faces looking at
camera do not. Faces date a site, and AI faces date it faster.

## 1 · Hero — the shop, wide

> Interior of a family-run musical instrument shop in Beirut at dusk, shot wide
> and slightly low. On the left wall, a row of hanging ouds and hand-inlaid
> mother-of-pearl darbukas. On the right, electric guitars and a stack of
> amplifiers. A worn wooden counter runs through the middle distance. Warm
> tungsten light pools from a single hanging bulb; the corners fall into
> near-black. Dust visible in the light. Nobody in frame. Cinematic, wide
> aspect, deep shadow, warm brown and brass tones only. Photorealistic
> editorial photography, 35mm, f/2, fine grain. No text, no signage, no logos.

Aspect **21:9**, at least 2600px wide. The headline sits over the left third, so
keep that side uncluttered and dark.

## Checking them before you upload

Three things will make an image fail on the site:

1. **Cool tones.** If it comes back grey or blue it will fight the brass and
   bone palette. Regenerate rather than colour-correct.
2. **A bright, even exposure.** The bands put cream text over these images. If
   the frame is bright everywhere the type disappears. Ask for "deep shadow,
   low-key" again.
3. **Invented text.** Image models like to write words on signs and amplifier
   grilles, and they are always nonsense. Zoom in before uploading.

Aim for roughly 300–500 KB per image after Shopify processes it; upload at full
size and let Shopify resize.
